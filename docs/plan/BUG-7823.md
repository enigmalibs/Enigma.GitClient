# BUG-7823 — A drag to the diff's end hangs the app

**Status:** DONE — see `docs/done/BUG-7823.md`
**Type:** BUG
**Branch:** `bugfix/bug-7823-drag-end-hang`
**Run:** bugfix/2026-10-05-diff-drag-hang

## Objective

Dragging a text selection in a diff down to its end — the pointer at the bottom-right, past the
last line — no longer freezes the application. Today:

- the I-beam cursor stays;
- the window cannot be closed with its close button;
- memory keeps climbing.

The selection reaches the end of the document and the editor settles there.

## Context & constraints

- **Where.** The diff editors are `DiffTextEditor` (FEATURE-BB60): the unified one and the two
  sides of the side-by-side rendering. Each is an AvaloniaEdit `TextEditor` with
  `AllowScrollBelowDocument = false`.
- **AvaloniaEdit 12's drag handler.** While a selection is being dragged, `SelectionMouseHandler`
  re-extends the selection on every `TextView.ScrollOffsetChanged`, then calls
  `Caret.BringCaretToView(5)`, which calls `TextView.MakeVisible(caret rectangle inflated by 5)`.
- **The unreachable offset.** `MakeVisible` sets the offset that shows the whole rectangle, clamped
  at 0 but not at the far end. At the end of the document that offset is past what the scroll
  viewer allows:
  - vertically, because nothing may scroll below the document;
  - sideways, because the extent is the widest line plus 3 px against a 5 px margin.
- **The evidence.** A headless probe, holding the drag at the corner:
  - the text view sits at `y = 1097.25` while its scroll viewer allows `1092.25`; sideways it is
    `2040.63` against `2038.13`;
  - every time the scroll viewer coerces its offset again, the drag handler re-extends, `MakeVisible`
    pushes past the limit again, and the two fight — 330 scroll events from six window nudges.
- **Why only on screen.** In a real window the scroll viewer re-coerces on every layout, so the fight
  never stops. Each round rebuilds the visual lines, which is where the memory goes. Headless only
  lays out when a frame is forced, which is why the existing drag tests passed.
- **The constructors.** `TextEditor(TextArea)` and `TextArea(TextView)` are protected, so a custom
  text view needs a `TextArea` subclass to carry it.

## Steps

1. `Controls/Diff/DiffTextView.cs` — `DiffTextView : TextView`, styled as a `TextView`. `MakeVisible`
   moves the requested rectangle inside what can be scrolled to — the extent, or the viewport when the
   content is smaller — before asking the base to show it. It is unchanged while nothing has been
   measured yet. `Reachable(rectangle, extent, viewport)` is the pure clamp.
2. `Controls/Diff/DiffTextArea.cs` — `DiffTextArea : TextArea`, styled as a `TextArea`, built on a
   `DiffTextView`.
3. `DiffTextEditor` is built on a `DiffTextArea`.
4. Tests:
   - in the unified view and both sides of the side-by-side one, a drag to the bottom-right corner,
     held while the viewer is made to coerce again (the window resized a pixel at a time), leaves the
     text view and its scroll viewer at the same offset with only a handful of scroll events;
   - the selection reaches the document's end;
   - `Reachable` keeps a rectangle that fits, moves one past the end back inside, and leaves one
     larger than the extent at the start;
   - the editor's text view is a `DiffTextView`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Dragging a selection past the bottom-right of any diff editor selects to the end of the document
  and the editor settles: its text view and its scroll viewer agree on the offset, and no scroll
  events follow once the pointer is still.
- Scrolling the caret into view still works everywhere else (keyboard, search, a drag in the middle).
- Build clean with zero warnings; the whole suite green.

## Out of scope

- Reporting the clamp to AvaloniaEdit upstream (a follow-up).
- Scrolling below the document's last line.

## Decisions taken autonomously

| Question | Chosen | Why | Alternatives rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where the fix goes | A `DiffTextView` whose `MakeVisible` asks only for reachable offsets | It removes the root cause — a target the scroll viewer cannot hold — on both axes and for every caller (drag, keyboard, search) | `AllowScrollBelowDocument = true` (fixes only the vertical axis, and changes how a diff scrolls); turning off scrolling during a drag (loses a feature that works) |
